## Where we've been

This book has taken you from a blank directory to a fully operational AI agent workforce that can research, write, edit, produce, and sell ebooks — all from a local-first architecture that respects your privacy and your pocket.

But technology doesn't stand still. The AI publishing landscape is evolving fast, and the Fair Dinkum workforce is designed to evolve with it.

## The roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation (current)

- 15 specialised agents covering the full ebook pipeline
- Local-first architecture with FastAPI MCP server
- Stripe integration for payments and subscriptions
- Obsidian vault publishing for knowledge management
- Docker deployment with CI/CD

### Phase 2: Expansion (next 6 months)

- **Audiobook agent:** Convert manuscripts to audiobook scripts with voice synthesis
- **Translation agent:** Localise ebooks for international markets (starting with US English)
- **Print-on-demand agent:** Generate print-ready interiors and covers for paperback distribution
- **Video summary agent:** Create short-form video content for social media promotion
- **Community agent:** Manage reader communities, collect feedback, and identify upsell opportunities

### Phase 3: Platform (6-12 months)

- **Multi-tenant architecture:** Let other authors run their own workforce instances
- **Template library:** Pre-built agent configurations for common genres (how-to, memoir, fiction, technical)
- **Marketplace:** Buy and sell agent prompts, style guides, and niche research
- **Analytics dashboard:** Web-based UI for monitoring projects across multiple workforces

### Phase 4: Ecosystem (12-24 months)

- **Integration marketplace:** Connect with third-party tools (email platforms, social schedulers, design tools)
- **Collaborative editing:** Real-time co-authoring between humans and AI agents
- **Quality certification:** Automated quality scoring that becomes a trust signal for readers
- **Industry benchmarks:** Anonymised aggregate data on what works in Australian self-publishing

## Community contributions

Open source only works with community. The Fair Dinkum workforce is designed for contribution:

### How to contribute

1. **Bug reports:** Found a problem? Open an issue with steps to reproduce.
2. **Feature requests:** Have an idea? Open an issue describing the use case.
3. **Agent contributions:** Built a new agent? Submit a pull request with tests.
4. **Documentation:** Found something unclear? Submit a docs improvement.
5. **Translations:** Help make the workforce accessible to non-English speakers.

### Contribution guidelines

Every contribution must:

- Include tests that verify the change works
- Not break existing tests (CI must pass)
- Follow the existing code style (Black formatting, type hints)
- Include documentation for any new public APIs
- Respect the local-first philosophy — no forced cloud dependencies

### Recognition

Contributors are recognised in three ways:

- **CONTRIBUTORS.md:** All contributors listed in the project root
- **Release notes:** Significant contributions highlighted in release announcements
- **Revenue share:** Contributors to revenue-generating features receive a percentage of net revenue for 12 months

## Monetisation models

The Fair Dinkum workforce is open source, but open source doesn't mean "can't make money." Several monetisation models are compatible with the architecture:

### Model 1: Consulting and setup

- **Setup package:** $500 for initial deployment and configuration

- **Monthly maintenance:** $200/month for updates, monitoring, and support

### Model 2: Training and courses

Teach other authors how to use the workforce effectively.

- **Online course:** $197 for a self-paced video course
- **Live workshops:** $497 for a weekend intensive
- **Corporate training:** Custom pricing for publishing companies

### Model 3: SaaS wrapper

Offer a hosted version for authors who don't want to self-host.

- **Starter:** $29/month — up to 3 projects, 5 agents
- **Professional:** $79/month — up to 20 projects, all agents
- **Enterprise:** $199/month — unlimited projects, custom agents, priority support

The SaaS wrapper uses the same open-source core but adds managed hosting, automatic backups, and premium support.

### Model 4: Marketplace fees

Take a percentage of transactions through the agent marketplace.

- **Template sales:** 30% platform fee on prompt and style guide sales
- **Research sales:** 20% platform fee on niche research packages
- **Agent sales:** 15% platform fee on custom agent sales

### Model 5: Certification and trust

Charge for quality certification that becomes a signal to readers.

- **Quality audit:** $100 per ebook — automated quality assessment with improvement recommendations
- **Certification badge:** $50/year — display a "Fair Dinkum Certified" badge on certified ebooks
- **Trust ranking:** Free for authors, licensing fee for platforms that want to display certification data

## Keeping it fair dinkum

As AI-generated content floods the market, trust becomes the scarcest resource. The Fair Dinkum philosophy is built on three principles:

### 1. Transparency

Every ebook produced by the workforce includes an "About this book" section that discloses:

- Which parts were AI-generated and which were human-edited
- What research sources were informed the content
- When the book was last updated

This isn't a weakness — it's a competitive advantage. Readers increasingly want to know what they're buying.

### 2. Quality over quantity

The workforce is designed to produce fewer, better books — not a flood of mediocre content. The editorial agent, fact-checker, and human gates exist specifically to prevent low-quality output from reaching readers.

If a book doesn't meet quality thresholds, it doesn't ship. Simple as that.

### 3. Author ownership

The workforce serves the author, not the platform. You own your content, your data, and your customer relationships. There's no exclusivity requirement, no platform lock-in, and no claim on your intellectual property.

If you want to take your content and leave, you can. All your data is exportable in open formats.

## The Australian angle

Australia has unique advantages in the AI publishing landscape:

### Time zone

When it's business hours in Australia, it's night in the US and morning in Europe. Australian authors can respond to US market trends overnight and European trends in the morning.

### English-speaking market

Australia is part of the global English-language market, which is the largest ebook market in the world. No translation needed for the biggest audience.

### Trusted brand

Australian products carry a quality perception globally. "Made in Australia" signals craftsmanship and authenticity — an advantage in a market flooded with cheap AI-generated content.

### Small but connected

Australia's small population means tight networks. Word-of-mouth travels fast. A few successful authors recommending the workforce can saturate the Australian self-publishing community quickly.
