# Chapter 12: Analytics & Launch

*Revenue tracking, A/B testing, platform selection, timing, and the go-to-market playbook.*

---

## The measurement problem

What you don't measure, you can't improve. But not all metrics are created equal. Vanity metrics (page views, social followers) feel good but don't drive decisions. Actionable metrics (conversion rate, revenue per customer, churn rate) tell you exactly what to do next.

The Analytics Agent focuses exclusively on actionable metrics.

## Revenue tracking

Three revenue metrics matter at different stages:

### 1. Gross Revenue

The total money coming in before any costs. Tracked daily, weekly, monthly.

```
Daily:    $147
Weekly:   $892
Monthly:  $3,247
YTD:      $18,430
```

### 2. Net Revenue

Gross revenue minus refunds, chargebacks, and Stripe fees. This is the number that matters for sustainability.

```
Gross:     $3,247
Refunds:   -$97 (3.0%)
Chargebacks: -$49 (1.5%)
Stripe fees: -$187 (5.8%)
Net:       $2,914
```

### 3. Revenue per Customer (RPC)

Total revenue divided by unique customers. Tells you the lifetime value trajectory.

```
Single Meet buyers:  $4.00 RPC
Weekly subscribers:  $27.00 RPC (average 3 weeks retained)
Monthly subscribers: $57.00 RPC (average 3 months retained)
```

## Churn analysis

For subscription products, churn is the silent killer. The Analytics Agent tracks three churn metrics:

### Gross churn

Percentage of subscribers who cancel in a given period.

```
Month 1: 45% (typical — trial users who don't convert)
Month 2: 15% (stabilising base)
Month 3: 8%  (loyal core emerging)
Month 4+: 5% (mature retention)
```

### Net churn

Gross churn minus upgrades and reactivations. Can be negative if upgrades exceed cancellations.

```
Gross churn:     -$340/month
Upgrades:        +$120/month
Reactivations:   +$60/month
Net churn:       -$160/month ← healthy trajectory
```

### Cohort retention

Tracking when customers leave reveals patterns:

```
Cohort        Month 1  Month 2  Month 3  Month 4
Jan 2026       100%     55%      42%      35%
Feb 2026       100%     58%      48%      —
Mar 2026       100%     62%      —        —
Apr 2026       100%     —        —        —
```

If Month 2 retention is improving month-over-month, your product is getting stickier.

## A/B testing

The Analytics Agent designs and evaluates A/B tests across the funnel:

### Test categories

1. **Headline tests:** Which landing page headline produces the highest conversion rate?
2. **Price tests:** Does $4.99 convert better than $3.99 or $5.99?
3. **Copy tests:** Does a problem-first or solution-first landing page convert better?
4. **Email tests:** Which subject line produces the highest open rate?
5. **CTA tests:** "Buy Now" vs "Get Instant Access" vs "Download Now"

### Test design

Every test needs:

- **Hypothesis:** "Changing X will increase Y by Z%"
- **Control:** The current version
- **Variant:** The proposed change
- **Sample size:** Minimum 100 conversions per variant for statistical significance
- **Duration:** Minimum 7 days to capture weekday/weekend variation
- **Success metric:** The single number that determines the winner

### Test results

The agent produces a test report with:

```
Test: Landing page headline
Control: "The Complete Guide to Native Plant Gardening"
Variant: "Grow a Native Garden That Thrives on Neglect"

Results:
  Control:   423 visitors, 31 conversions (7.3%)
  Variant:   418 visitors, 47 conversions (11.2%)
  
  Lift: +53.4%
  Confidence: 95.2%
  Recommendation: Ship the variant
```

## Platform selection

Not all sales platforms are equal. The Launch Strategist evaluates:

### Primary platforms

| Platform | Revenue share | Audience size | Control |
|----------|---------------|---------------|---------|
| Own website (Stripe) | 100% - fees | Your traffic | Full |
| Amazon KDP | 35-65% | Massive | Limited |
| Gumroad | 10% growing | Creator-focused | Moderate |
| Payhip | 5%+ | Small | Good |
| Apple Books | 30% | Apple ecosystem | Limited |

### Recommended launch sequence

1. **Day 1-7:** Launch on own website (highest margin, full control)
2. **Day 8-14:** Expand to Gumroad (creator audience, easy setup)
3. **Day 15-30:** Publish on Amazon KDP (massive audience, lower margin)
4. **Day 31+:** Evaluate Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play

This sequence maximises revenue early while building toward maximum reach.

## Timing

Launch timing affects initial momentum, which affects platform algorithms, which affects long-term sales.

### Weekday patterns

```
Best days to launch: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
Best days to send email: Tuesday, Thursday
Worst days: Friday afternoon, Saturday, Sunday
```

### Seasonal patterns

For a gardening ebook:

```
Peak: March-May (autumn planting), September-November (spring planting)
Trough: June-August (winter), December-January (summer heat)
```

### Industry events

Launch alongside relevant events:

- Australian Garden Show Sydney (March)
- Melbourne International Flower and Garden Show (March-April)
- Gardening Australia Ferepo (anytime — align with TV seasons)

## Launch playbook

The Launch Strategist produces a 30-day plan:

### Pre-launch (Days -14 to 0)

- [ ] Landing page live with email capture
- [ ] Free chapter opt-in magnet active
- [ ] Beta reader testimonials collected
- [ ] Email sequence loaded and tested
- [ ] Social media accounts primed with pre-launch content

### Launch week (Days 1-7)

- [ ] Day 1: Launch announcement to email list
- [ ] Day 2: Social media launch posts
- [ ] Day 3: First reader testimonial shared
- [ ] Day 4: Podcast guest appearance or guest post
- [ ] Day 5: Limited-time launch bonus announced
- [ [Day 6: Email sequence continues
- [ ] Day 7: Launch week wrap-up, bonus expiry warning

### Post-launch (Days 8-30)

- [ ] Expand to Gumroad and Amazon
- [ ] Collect and publish reviews
- [ ] Run first A/B test on landing page
- [ ] Analyse Week 1 data and adjust email sequence
- [ ] Plan next book based on reader feedback

## Key takeaways

- Track gross revenue, net revenue, and revenue per customer.
- Measure churn by type: gross, net, and cohort retention.
- Design A/B tests with clear hypotheses and statistical significance.
- Launch on your own site first, then expand to marketplaces.
- Timing matters: weekday, seasonal, and event alignment.
- A 30-day launch playbook coordinates all moving parts.
