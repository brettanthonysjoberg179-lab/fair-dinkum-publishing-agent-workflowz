About Fair Dinkum Publishing

Adelaide, South Australia

We're a small digital publishing company based in Adelaide, South Australia. We build practical guides, templates, and tools for Australian small business owners who need to navigate AI, compliance, and modern publishing — without the enterprise price tag.

Our Mission

Most business content is written for US or EU audiences. We're building something different: Australian-specific, practical, and honest. No fluff, no filler, no 47-page whitepapers that gather dust. Just fair dinkum content you can use today.

What We Publish

  • Templates — Privacy policies, AI-use policies, checklists
  • Workflow tools — Publishing automation, agent templates, Drive organization
