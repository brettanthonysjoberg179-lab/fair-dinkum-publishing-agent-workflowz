Why Australian Small Business Needs Better AI Tools

September 21, 2026

Most AI guides are written for a US or EU audience. They reference GDPR, CCPA, or HIPAA. They assume you have a legal team, a compliance officer, and a budget for enterprise tools.

Australian small business owners don't have any of that. You're a sole trader, a tradie, or a small team juggling everything yourself. You need practical, locally-relevant advice — not a 47-page whitepaper on EU AI regulation.

The Gap

There's a massive gap in the market for Australian-specific AI guidance. Consider:

  • The Privacy Act 1988 has unique requirements (APPs) that don't map neatly to GDPR
  • OAIC enforcement is different from EU data protection authorities
  • Australian contract law, consumer law, and employment law all interact with AI in specific ways
  • Most "AI compliance" content online is written by US firms for US audiences

What We're Building

Fair Dinkum Publishing exists to fill this gap. We build digital products — guides, templates, tools — that are:

  • Australian-specific — written for our laws, our regulators, our business culture
  • Practical — not theoretical frameworks, but actual templates and checklists you can use today
  • Honest — we tell you what we don't know, what's changed, and what's still uncertain

Our First Product

We're launching with The AU AI Compliance Playbook: Small Business Guide to Privacy Act 1988 + Automated Decisions. It's a comprehensive guide for Australian small businesses who need to understand their AI privacy obligations — without hiring a law firm.

It includes:

  • A plain-English breakdown of APP 1.7 and automated decision-making rules
  • Real OAIC enforcement examples (what actually happens when you get it wrong)
  • Ready-to-use privacy policy templates with AI disclosure clauses
  • A 90-day compliance checklist you can follow step-by-step
